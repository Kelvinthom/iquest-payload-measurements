Engineering implications

Payload growth: Increasing from 4,096 to 65,536 samples produces roughly 16 times the original data and 15.2 times the compressed data.

Compression: All five tests show approximately 62–64% reduction. Compression helps bandwidth usage but does not remove the message-size constraint.

Azure IoT Hub: 8,192 is the largest tested sample count whose compressed payload is below the 256 KB device-to-cloud message limit. At 16,384 and above, even the compressed payload exceeds the limit. Consider splitting messages or uploading larger captures as files.

Signal analysis: At a fixed sampling frequency, more samples increase capture duration and improve FFT bin spacing. This study does not measure signal quality, latency, CPU usage or successful cloud delivery.

Sizes are reported exactly as displayed in Node-RED. Actual message size may include metadata or encoding overhead; the screenshots do not establish successful ingestion. Azure limit: Microsoft documentation.
